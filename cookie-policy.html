What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember certain information about your visit — such as your language preference or whether you have already seen a consent notice.

Your choices

When you first visit this site, a banner gives you the option to accept or decline cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this domain, which will cause the banner to reappear on your next visit.

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. See your browser's help documentation for instructions. Note that refusing all cookies will not meaningfully affect your use of this site, as we use no functional or tracking cookies beyond the consent preference itself.

Legal basis

Our use of the ck_cookie_consent cookie is based on our legitimate interest in not repeatedly displaying the consent banner to the same visitor. No personal data is stored in this cookie. Where we process personal data submitted via contact forms, we do so on the basis of your consent at the point of submission.
