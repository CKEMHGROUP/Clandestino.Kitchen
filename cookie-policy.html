2. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website when you visit it. Cookies are widely used to make websites work, to improve their efficiency, and to provide information to the owners of the site. Some cookies are placed by the website you are visiting; others are placed by third-party services that operate on that website.

Cookies may be session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them.

3. Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies across our websites.

Strictly necessary cookies are essential for our websites to function. They enable core features such as page navigation, access to secure areas, and the operation of our booking and enquiry forms. These cookies do not collect information that could be used to identify you for marketing purposes. Because they are essential, they are placed without requiring your consent.

Analytics cookies allow us to understand how visitors interact with our websites. They collect information about which pages are visited, how long visitors spend on each page, and where visitors come from. This information is aggregated and used to improve the performance and content of our websites. Analytics cookies are placed only with your consent.

Functionality cookies allow our websites to remember choices you make, such as your language preference or consent settings. These cookies improve your experience and are placed only with your consent.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. We do not allow third parties to place advertising cookies on our websites.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our websites are placed by third-party services we use. These include Stripe, Inc. for payment processing, and any analytics provider active on our websites. Third-party cookies are subject to those providers’ own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control third-party cookies and are not responsible for the content of third-party policies.

We will update this section with the names of active third-party analytics providers as they are confirmed. If you have questions about third-party cookies in the meantime, please contact us at compliance@clandestino.kitchen.

5. Cookie Retention Periods

Strictly necessary cookies are session cookies and are deleted when you close your browser, unless they are required to persist your consent or security settings, in which case they are retained for up to twelve months. Analytics cookies are retained for up to twenty-six months from the date they are placed. Functionality cookies are retained for up to twelve months.

6. Your Choices

When you first visit any CK LLC website, you will be presented with a cookie consent mechanism that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by revisiting the cookie consent mechanism on the relevant website.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our websites. Disabling analytics or functionality cookies will not prevent you from using our websites but may affect your experience.

Detailed guidance on managing cookies in the most commonly used browsers is available at the browser provider’s support pages.
