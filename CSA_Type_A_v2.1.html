Recitals

CK LLC owns and operates JoEs TaBLe, a corporate event management and culinary concept brand operating globally. The Chef is an independent professional culinary contractor operating globally. CK LLC engages the Chef as an independent international service provider to supply professional culinary management, menu concept execution, and culinary event direction for JoEs TaBLe Events worldwide. The parties wish to establish a global framework agreement governing all such engagements, ensuring absolute legal, operational, health, labor, and tax liability insulation for CK LLC.

1. Definitions

1.1. An "Event" means a corporate experience or culinary activation delivered under the JoEs TaBLe brand anywhere in the world, as confirmed in a Service Order issued by CK LLC.

1.2. A "Service Order" means a written or electronic document issued by CK LLC to the Chef specifying the event date, host country/jurisdiction, venue, guest count, creative brief, agreed fee, and operational parameters for a specific Event.

1.3. "Host Jurisdiction" means the specific country, state, territory, province, or municipality where the physical preparation or execution of an Event takes place.

1.4. The "Brand Standards" means concept presentation guidelines, plating aesthetics, service conduct expectations, and experiential narratives communicated by CK LLC for an Event.

1.5. The "Menu Concept" means any tasting menu, course concept, recipe narrative, or culinary structure developed by or for CK LLC in connection with JoEs TaBLe Events.

2. Scope of Services & Absolute Operational Separation

2.1. Independent Engagement: CK LLC engages the Chef as an independent professional contractor to provide culinary consultation, menu implementation, and production direction directly to CK LLC. The Chef’s services are provided strictly B2B to CK LLC. The Chef does not render services to, nor enter into any commercial or contractual relationship with, any end guest, attendee, or event sponsor.

2.2. Global Per-Event Scope: This agreement establishes a global framework. CK LLC has no obligation to guarantee a minimum volume of Events. Each Event shall be governed by a separate Service Order.

2.3. Exclusion of Facilities & Logistics: CK LLC does not own, lease, manage, supervise, inspect, or operate any kitchen, food preparation space, cold-storage facility, transport vehicle, or culinary equipment in any Host Jurisdiction. The Chef is solely and exclusively responsible for securing, inspecting, and utilizing fully licensed, sanitary, and legally compliant production premises and equipment.

3. Global Health, Sanitary & Food Safety Compliance

3.1. Local & International Compliance: The Chef warrants that all ingredient sourcing, transportation, cold-chain maintenance, storage, preparation, cooking, holding, allergen management, and service strictly comply with: (a) all applicable public health, food hygiene, and sanitary laws of the Host Jurisdiction; (b) recognized international food safety standards (including HACCP / APPCC protocols and Codex Alimentarius guidelines); and (c) mandatory allergen disclosure regulations applicable in the Host Jurisdiction (including EU Regulation 1169/2011 where applicable, FDA food labeling guidelines, or local equivalents).

3.2. Universal Certification Warranty: The Chef warrants that they and all members of their culinary team hold all valid food handler certificates, health permits, and professional licenses required by the Host Jurisdiction. Proof of certification shall be submitted to CK LLC prior to the execution of any Service Order.

3.3. Traceability & Allergen Control: The Chef retains sole operational responsibility for maintaining food temperature logs, preventing cross-contamination, managing guest dietary restriction notifications, and maintaining ingredient origin traceability records.

4. Brand Standards vs. Operational Autonomy

4.1. Scope of Brand Standards: CK LLC’s Brand Standards govern exclusively visual aesthetics, concept narrative, plating alignment, service timing, and guest experience design.

4.2. Complete Operational Independence: Brand Standards do not control or direct—and shall never be interpreted as controlling or directing—kitchen operations, food handling methods, raw ingredient selection, sanitation, or physical culinary execution. The Chef retains total operational autonomy over the mechanics of food production.

5. Total Liability Allocation, Indemnity & Hold Harmless

5.1. Sole Operational Responsibility: The Chef retains 100% exclusive legal, civil, criminal, regulatory, and financial liability for food safety, toxic food exposures, foodborne illnesses, allergic reactions, kitchen accidents, physical property damage, personal injury, and sanitary infractions occurring at or in connection with any Event.

5.2. Absolute Indemnification: To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Chef shall defend, indemnify, and hold completely harmless CK LLC, its parent entities, officers, directors, members, managers, employees, agents, and brand affiliates from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, penalties, fines, administrative sanctions, costs, and legal fees (including attorney fees) arising directly or indirectly from: (a) the Chef’s or the Chef's team's performance, negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct; (b) food poisoning, allergic reactions, illness, death, or bodily injury linked to food, beverage, or services prepared or provided by the Chef; (c) failure of the Chef or the Chef’s team/subcontractors to maintain required licenses, permits, tax registrations, or insurance in any Host Jurisdiction; (d) any damage to venue premises, leased kitchens, or third-party equipment; or (e) any dispute, audit, fine, or sanction brought by health, municipal, labor, tax, or social security authorities in any Host Jurisdiction against the Chef or CK LLC relating to the Chef's services.

5.3. Limitation of Liability: CK LLC shall not be liable to the Chef or any third party for indirect, incidental, consequential, punitive, or special damages. CK LLC’s total aggregate liability under any circumstances arising out of or related to a Service Order shall be strictly limited to the direct contract fee paid to the Chef under that specific Service Order.

6. Insurance Requirements

6.1. Mandatory Policy Maintenance: The Chef shall maintain in full force at their own cost throughout the term of this agreement: (a) Public & General Liability Insurance covering third-party bodily injury, property damage, and product/foodborne liability with a minimum insured sum of EUR 300,000 / USD 350,000 (or local equivalent) per occurrence; and (b) Professional Indemnity Insurance appropriate for global culinary consultation.

6.2. Proof of Coverage: The Chef shall provide CK LLC with an active Certificate of Insurance naming CK LLC as an Additional Insured (where permitted by the insurer) prior to providing services under any Service Order.

7. Tax Status, Cross-Border Invoicing & B2B Localization

7.1. Non-EU / Cross-Border B2B Supply: CK LLC is a legal entity incorporated in the State of Wyoming, USA, and maintains no permanent establishment outside the United States. All services rendered by the Chef are B2B cross-border professional services supplied to a non-resident US corporate entity.

7.2. Global Tax Invoicing Rules: (a) For Chefs/Services originating within the European Union: The supply is categorized as a B2B service to a non-EU recipient localized outside the EU (e.g., Art. 69 Ley 37/1992 in Spain or equivalent EU VAT Directive provisions). Invoices shall state: "Operación no sujeta a IVA por reglas de localización — Art. 69 Ley 37/1992 / Cross-border B2B supply to non-EU entity." No VAT shall be charged. (b) For Chefs/Services originating outside the European Union: Invoices shall be issued in accordance with local export/cross-border service tax regulations without local VAT/GST/sales tax additions.

7.3. Zero Withholding Tax: CK LLC is a non-resident corporate entity and will not act as a foreign tax withholding agent. The Chef is solely responsible for declaring all income and paying all personal, corporate, income, self-employment, and sales taxes in their home tax jurisdiction and any applicable Host Jurisdiction.

8. Independent Contractor & Labor Shield

8.1. Global Independent Status: The Chef is engaged solely as an independent professional contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates or implies an employment contract, agency relationship, joint venture, statutory partnership, or franchise between CK LLC and the Chef.

8.2. Labor & Subcontractor Insulation: If the Chef engages any assistants, sub-chefs, kitchen staff, or helpers for an Event, such individuals are exclusively employed or contracted by the Chef. The Chef assumes 100% legal, financial, and regulatory responsibility for their compensation, workplace safety, labor law compliance, worker's compensation, social security contributions, and visa/work authorization permits in the Host Jurisdiction. CK LLC has zero employment relationship with the Chef’s personnel and is completely shielded from joint labor liability claims globally.

9. Intellectual Property & Brand Protection

9.1. Ownership of Menu Concepts: All Menu Concepts, recipe pairings, tasting narratives, presentation formats, and creative deliverables developed by the Chef for JoEs TaBLe Events shall be the exclusive intellectual property of CK LLC globally from the moment of creation.

9.2. Public Communication & NDAs: The Chef shall keep strictly confidential all client details, pricing, attendee identities, and operational models. The Chef shall not publish photographs, client logos, or promotional references regarding JoEs TaBLe Events without prior written authorization from CK LLC.

10. Term and Termination

10.1. Framework Term: This framework agreement commences upon signature and continues until terminated by either party upon fourteen (14) calendar days' written notice.

10.2. Immediate Termination for Cause: CK LLC may terminate this agreement and any pending Service Order immediately without penalty if: (a) the Chef fails to maintain required food safety permits, professional licenses, or insurance; (b) the Chef causes a public health risk, sanitary breach, or severe reputational damage; (c) the Chef fails to comply with local tax, labor, or immigration laws in any Host Jurisdiction; or (d) the Chef materially breaches any provision of this agreement.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1. Wyoming Governing Law: This agreement, its interpretation, validity, and all contractual or extra-contractual claims arising between CK LLC and the Chef shall be governed exclusively by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

11.2. Exclusive Forum: The parties irrevocably agree that any suit, action, or legal proceeding arising out of or relating to this agreement shall be brought exclusively in the State or Federal Courts located in the State of Wyoming, USA. The Chef explicitly waives any objection to personal jurisdiction or venue in such courts.

11.3. Local Operational Compliance Disclaimer: Notwithstanding Clause 11.1, the Chef acknowledges that physical operations performed within any Host Jurisdiction must strictly observe mandatory local public health, safety, and sanitary laws, for which the Chef assumes sole legal liability.
