Recitals
CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC owns and operates JoEs TaBLe, a corporate event management and culinary concept brand operating globally. The Venue Partner operates a licensed dining venue holding all licences and authorisations required for the preparation and service of food and beverages on its premises. The parties wish to establish the terms under which the Venue Partner will provide venue and operational services to support the delivery of JoEs TaBLe Concept Experiences.

1. Definitions

1.1 A "Concept Experience" means a private or corporate experience produced under the JoEs TaBLe brand, delivered at the Venue Partner's premises, as confirmed in writing by CK LLC.

1.2 A "Service Order" means a written confirmation issued by CK LLC specifying the date, guest count, menu, agreed fee, and any additional requirements for a specific Concept Experience.

1.3 The "Base Fee" means the fixed per-person fee payable by CK LLC to the Venue Partner for each Concept Experience, as set out in Clause 4.

1.4 "Brand Standards" means the concept presentation, service conduct, table setting, and experiential guidelines communicated by CK LLC prior to each Concept Experience.

2. Scope of Services

2.1 The Venue Partner agrees to make its licensed premises available for JoEs TaBLe Concept Experiences on dates confirmed via Service Order. The premises shall be made available exclusively for the Concept Experience during the agreed period.

2.2 The Venue Partner shall provide qualified on-site service staff for each Concept Experience. Staff conduct and presentation shall meet the Brand Standards communicated by CK LLC.

2.3 The Venue Partner shall make its kitchen facilities available to the culinary team appointed by CK LLC. Kitchen access, utilities, and basic equipment are included in the Base Fee.

2.4 The Venue Partner shall obtain and maintain all licences, permits, and certifications required by applicable law for the operation of a food service establishment. CK LLC bears no responsibility for any regulatory compliance obligation relating to the premises or food service operation.

3. Brand and Presentation

3.1 Concept Experiences delivered at the Venue Partner's premises operate exclusively under the JoEs TaBLe brand. The Venue Partner shall not co-brand or present the Concept Experience under its own trading name without the prior written consent of CK LLC.

3.2 CK LLC shall communicate Brand Standards no fewer than 48 hours before each Concept Experience. The Venue Partner shall ensure that table setting, ambient presentation, and service conduct conform to those standards.

3.3 Client relationships, bookings, and all correspondence with clients are the exclusive property of CK LLC. The Venue Partner shall not contact clients directly without prior written consent of CK LLC.

4. Fees and Payment

4.1 CK LLC shall pay the Venue Partner a Base Fee of EUR 75.00 per guest per Concept Experience. The Base Fee covers exclusive use of the licensed premises, kitchen access and facilities, on-site service staff, and all utilities for the Concept Experience duration. No additional charges apply unless agreed in writing in the relevant Service Order.

4.4 Where CK LLC applies a corporate surcharge to a Concept Experience, the Base Fee payable to the Venue Partner remains EUR 75.00 per person regardless of the surcharge applied.

4.5 CK LLC shall pay the Venue Partner within fourteen days of the Concept Experience date, upon receipt of a valid invoice.

5. Brand Standards Scope Limitation

5.1 CK LLC's Brand Standards do not govern the structural condition of the premises, venue safety, fire safety compliance, food service hygiene, or any matter that is the subject of the Venue Partner's autonomous regulatory obligations as a licensed food service establishment. Compliance with all applicable regulations governing licensed premises is the Venue Partner's sole and exclusive responsibility.

6. Liability and Indemnity

6.1 The Venue Partner is solely responsible for the safety, maintenance, and regulatory compliance of the premises at all times, including during Concept Experiences.

6.2 The Venue Partner shall indemnify and hold harmless CK LLC from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, costs, and liabilities arising from: the condition of the premises; any failure to hold or maintain required licences, permits, or certifications; any act or omission of the Venue Partner's staff; or any claim by a guest or third party arising from the physical condition or operation of the premises.

6.3 CK LLC is not liable to the Venue Partner for indirect, consequential, or economic losses. Nothing in this clause limits either party's liability for death or personal injury caused by that party's own negligence.

7. Insurance

7.1 The Venue Partner shall hold and maintain public liability insurance covering third-party personal injury and property damage with a minimum insured sum of EUR 600,000 per Concept Experience, consistent with the requirements of the Venue Partner's operating licence and applicable regulations on licensed premises.

7.2 The Venue Partner shall provide CK LLC with a current certificate of insurance before the first Concept Experience and within five business days of each annual renewal. CK LLC shall be named as an additional insured on the Venue Partner's public liability policy.

8. VAT and Invoicing

8.1 The Venue Partner shall invoice CK LLC for the Base Fee and any applicable pairing fees in accordance with applicable local tax law. The Venue Partner is responsible for the correct application of any local VAT or IVA on its invoices to CK LLC.

8.2 CK LLC invoices its clients independently in accordance with its own tax obligations as a US-incorporated entity.

9. Performance Standard

9.1 Where the Venue Partner achieves an average guest rating of 4.8 or above across all Concept Experiences in a calendar quarter, CK LLC shall pay a performance bonus of EUR 200.00 per qualifying quarter within thirty days of the end of that quarter.

10. Exclusivity and Non-Solicitation

10.1 The Venue Partner agrees not to independently contact, solicit, or accept bookings from clients introduced to it through JoEs TaBLe Concept Experiences during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve months following termination.

10.2 Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Venue Partner from operating its own food and beverage business independently of CK LLC.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all pricing, client information, and operational details of the other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent.

12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement commences on acceptance and continues indefinitely until terminated by either party on thirty days' written notice. Termination does not affect rights or obligations that have accrued prior to the date of termination.

12.2 CK LLC may terminate immediately if the Venue Partner loses or fails to maintain any required licence, fails to maintain required insurance, or materially breaches any obligation and fails to remedy the breach within fourteen days of written notice.

13. Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, United States of America. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wyoming, USA. Nothing in this clause limits either party's rights under mandatory applicable local laws.
