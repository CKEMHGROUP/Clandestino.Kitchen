The group

A hospitality group
built on food culture

CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is the parent entity for a portfolio of brands that share one conviction: that the most powerful human experiences happen around a table.

We design and produce private culinary events for the world's leading corporations and discerning private clients. We consult for hotels, restaurants, and F&B operators on concept, strategy, and execution. And we have built the proprietary technology that powers our operations.

Philosophy

Food is not
a product.
It is culture.

"Food culture is one of the most important pillars of civilization. An extension of our nationalistic feeling, our ethnic feeling, our personal history, our province, our region, our tribe, our origins. Food is everything we are."
Anthony Bourdain · The conviction behind everything we build

Every brand in the CK group shares one foundational belief: the most meaningful experiences — in business and in life — happen around a table. We do not sell meals. We design occasions. We build the cultural and commercial infrastructure that makes extraordinary hospitality possible.

Vision & roadmap

Building a global
hospitality brand

CK is building the number one global corporate culinary event brand — with clean auditable financials, a co-op execution network across multiple cities, and recurring revenue from technology licensing.

The group is structured for acquisition by a hospitality group, luxury travel company, food conglomerate, or private equity roll-up. Target exit: €8–20M at full build-out.
