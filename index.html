About CK LLC

A non-operational
management
entity.

CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is a global event management and hospitality consultancy incorporated in Wyoming, USA. We design premium culinary experiences and manage international corporate dining events through a network of carefully curated venue and hospitality partners.

CK LLC does not own venues, operate restaurants, employ hospitality staff, or hold food service licenses. All physical event execution and hospitality services are delivered exclusively by our independent network of licensed, locally-registered venue partners and culinary professionals.

CK LLC operates as an event broker and hospitality consultant. All revenue flows through a single USD account at Mercury Bank. All international invoicing is conducted in EUR.

Business model

Two revenue channels.
One entity.

CK LLC operates two distinct revenue channels under a single legal entity and a single bank account. All B2B client invoicing is under reverse charge. CK LLC does not collect or remit local VAT, IVA, or sales tax.

B2C
Consumer culinary
experiences

CK LLC designs and licenses a premium culinary event format under the JoEs TaBLe brand. Consumers purchase access to these curated experiences directly from CK LLC. A licensed, independent venue or chef partner executes each event under JoEs TaBLe brand standards. The consumer's contract is exclusively with CK LLC.

Partner network model

Asset-light.
Compliant.
Scalable.

CK LLC's operational model depends entirely on a global network of independent, licensed hospitality professionals and venue operators. We do not employ these partners. They operate as independent contractors under service agreements with CK LLC.

Each partner maintains their own business licenses, food service permits, liability insurance, and local tax registration. Each partner invoices CK LLC for services rendered and has no direct contractual relationship with end consumers or corporate clients.

This structure ensures CK LLC operates as a pure management and advisory entity with no operational liability tied to local jurisdiction food service, employment, or hospitality regulations.

Regulatory and compliance framework

Built for
due diligence.

01
US federal compliance

Annual Form 5472 filing with the IRS. US federal disregarded entity status (single-member LLC). Non-US sourced income not subject to US federal income tax. 10-year audit trail maintained on all transaction records.

02
International VAT compliance

All EU B2B invoicing under Art. 44 reverse charge rules (EU VAT Directive). VIES validation conducted before each EU B2B invoice. Spanish autonomo and EU vendor payments under Art. 69 place-of-supply rules. Zero IVA on all inbound partner invoices.

03
Partner compliance obligations

All executing partners hold valid food service and venue operation licenses in their respective jurisdictions. Partners maintain comprehensive liability and professional indemnity insurance. Partners comply with local employment, health and safety, and food safety regulations independently.
