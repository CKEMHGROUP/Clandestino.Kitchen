Business model

Two channels.
One entity.

Consumer
Culinary experiences

We design and license a premium culinary event format under the JoEs TaBLe brand. Guests purchase access to curated experiences directly. A licensed, independent venue or chef partner executes each event to our brand standard.

Corporate
Event management & consultancy

We provide comprehensive event management and hospitality advisory services to corporate clients globally, under the Clandestino Kitchen identity. We design bespoke dining experiences and coordinate every aspect of delivery.
