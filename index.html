About CK LLC

A global event
management
consultancy.

CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is a global event management and hospitality consultancy incorporated in Wyoming, USA. We design premium culinary experiences and manage international corporate dining events through a network of carefully curated venue and hospitality partners.

CK LLC operates as an event broker and hospitality consultant. All revenue flows through a single USD account at Mercury Bank. All international invoicing is conducted in EUR.

Business model

Two revenue channels.
One entity.

CK LLC operates two distinct revenue channels under a single legal entity and a single bank account.

B2C
Consumer culinary
experiences

CK LLC designs and licenses a premium culinary event format under the JoEs TaBLe brand. Consumers purchase access to these curated experiences directly from CK LLC. A licensed, independent venue or chef partner executes each event under JoEs TaBLe brand standards.

B2B
Corporate event management
& consultancy

CK LLC provides comprehensive event management and hospitality advisory services to corporate clients globally under the Clandestino Kitchen identity. We design bespoke corporate dining experiences, manage event logistics, and coordinate with venue and culinary partners.

Partner network

Asset-light.
Scalable.

CK LLC's operational model is built on a global network of independent, licensed hospitality professionals and venue operators. Each partner brings local expertise, licensing, and execution capability. CK LLC designs and curates the event experience, manages the client relationship, coordinates partner execution, and retains operational margin and format ownership.

This structure lets CK LLC scale rapidly into new markets without the overhead of direct operations, while maintaining one consistent standard across every event, everywhere.
