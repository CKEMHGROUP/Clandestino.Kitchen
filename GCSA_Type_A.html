Recitals and Electronic Acceptance
This Global Master Chef Services Agreement establishes a binding legal framework between CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC, trading as Clandestino Kitchen and JoEs TaBLe, and you as the independent culinary contractor identified in Step 1 of this onboarding submission. By checking the acceptance box during the onboarding process, you electronically consent and agree to all terms set forth herein, which shall govern all future Service Orders issued to you by CK LLC.

1. Definitions

1.1 A "Concept Experience" means a corporate or private experience delivered under the JoEs TaBLe brand anywhere in the world, as confirmed in a Service Order.

1.2 A "Service Order" means a written or electronic document issued by CK LLC specifying the date, host country and jurisdiction, venue, guest count, brief, agreed fee, and operational parameters for a Concept Experience.

1.3 "Host Jurisdiction" means the specific country, state, territory, province, or municipality where physical food preparation or service execution takes place.

1.4 "Brand Standards" means concept presentation guidelines, plating aesthetics, service conduct, and experiential narratives communicated by CK LLC.

2. Scope of Services and Operational Separation

2.1 You are engaged solely as an independent professional contractor providing B2B culinary consultation, menu execution, and event direction directly to CK LLC. You do not provide services to, nor maintain any direct commercial or contractual relationship with, any end guest, attendee, or event client.

2.2 CK LLC does not own, lease, operate, manage, supervise, or inspect any kitchen, food preparation space, cold storage, transport vehicle, or culinary equipment in any Host Jurisdiction. You are solely and exclusively responsible for securing, inspecting, and operating fully licensed, sanitary, and legally compliant production premises and equipment.

2.3 CK LLC engages you on a per-Concept Experience basis. This Agreement establishes the framework. Each engagement is governed by a separate Service Order.

3. Global Health, Sanitary and Food Safety Compliance

3.1 You warrant that all ingredient sourcing, transportation, cold-chain maintenance, storage, preparation, cooking, holding, allergen management, and service strictly comply with: (a) all applicable public health and food hygiene laws of the Host Jurisdiction; (b) international hygiene standards including HACCP and APPCC protocols; and (c) mandatory allergen disclosure regulations applicable in the Host Jurisdiction, including EU Regulation 1169/2011 or local equivalents.

3.2 You warrant that you and all members of your culinary team hold valid food handler certificates, health permits, and professional registrations required by the Host Jurisdiction. You shall supply copies of these certificates to CK LLC upon request and before the first Concept Experience.

3.3 You retain sole operational responsibility for food temperature logs, allergen cross-contamination prevention, dietary restriction enforcement, and ingredient origin traceability records.

4. Brand Standards and Operational Autonomy

4.1 CK LLC's Brand Standards govern exclusively visual aesthetics, concept narrative, plating alignment, service timing, and guest experience design.

4.2 Brand Standards do not control or direct — and shall never be interpreted as controlling or directing — kitchen operations, food safety procedures, raw ingredient selection, sanitation, or physical food production mechanics. You retain complete operational autonomy over all food safety practices.

5. Liability, Indemnity and Hold Harmless

5.1 You retain exclusive legal, civil, regulatory, and financial liability for food safety, foodborne illnesses, allergic reactions, kitchen accidents, personal injury, physical property damage, and sanitary infractions arising from your services.

5.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, you shall defend, indemnify, and hold completely harmless CK LLC, its officers, directors, members, employees, agents, and brand affiliates from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, penalties, fines, administrative sanctions, and legal costs arising from: (a) your negligence or wilful misconduct; (b) food poisoning, allergic reactions, or injury suffered by any guest or third party; (c) failure to maintain required health permits, tax registrations, or insurance; (d) damage to venue premises or equipment; or (e) any labour, health, or tax audit or fine brought by local authorities in any Host Jurisdiction relating to your services.

5.3 CK LLC's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to any Service Order shall not exceed the direct contract fee paid to you under that specific Service Order. CK LLC is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

6. Insurance Requirements

6.1 You shall maintain in full force: (a) public and general liability insurance covering third-party bodily injury, property damage, and foodborne product liability with a minimum limit of EUR 300,000 per occurrence; and (b) professional indemnity insurance covering claims arising from your culinary services.

6.2 You shall supply an active certificate of insurance naming CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC as an additional insured prior to performing under any Service Order and within five business days of each annual renewal.

6.3 CK LLC reserves the right to suspend any Service Order until compliant insurance cover is confirmed.

7. Professional Registration Warranty

7.1 You warrant that your professional activity is registered under the applicable business, trade, or professional classification in your home jurisdiction appropriate to independent culinary or professional services. You specifically confirm that your registration does not classify your activity as a restaurant, catering, or food service operator, or any equivalent classification in your jurisdiction.

7.2 You shall notify CK LLC immediately if your professional registration or classification changes during the term of this Agreement.

8. Tax Status and Cross-Border Invoicing

8.1 CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is incorporated in Wyoming, USA, with no permanent establishment outside the United States. Services rendered by you are B2B professional services supplied to a US entity.

8.2 You shall invoice CK LLC in accordance with the cross-border invoicing rules applicable in your own jurisdiction for services supplied to a US recipient. CK LLC will not act as a tax withholding agent in any jurisdiction. You are solely responsible for the correct tax treatment of your invoices and for declaring and paying all taxes applicable to your income in your home jurisdiction. The correct treatment is your responsibility — verify with your own accountant before issuing.

8.3 Invoices to CK LLC shall include your full legal name, your tax identification number, your registered address, the service description referencing the relevant Concept Experience, the agreed fee, and your applicable tax treatment. CK LLC will provide its EIN upon request for your compliance records.

9. Independent Contractor Status and Subcontractor Insulation

9.1 You act strictly as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, joint venture, or statutory partnership relationship.

9.2 Any assistants, sub-chefs, or kitchen staff engaged by you for a Concept Experience are exclusively employed or contracted by you. You assume full legal, financial, and administrative responsibility for their compensation, workplace safety, social security contributions, tax withholdings, and work permits. CK LLC is completely shielded from any joint labour, social security, or cesión ilegal de trabajadores claims globally.

10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Any Menu Concept, recipe pairing, tasting narrative, or presentation format developed by you in connection with a JoEs TaBLe Concept Experience is the exclusive intellectual property of CK LLC from the date of creation. You assign all intellectual property rights in any such concept to CK LLC.

10.2 You shall not represent any JoEs TaBLe concept, event format, or brand element as your own work in any public, commercial, or promotional context.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 You shall keep confidential all client information, pricing, event details, guest identities, and operational procedures of CK LLC and shall not make any public statement, post, or publication referencing JoEs TaBLe clients, event locations, or guest identities without prior written authorisation from CK LLC.

12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement commences upon electronic acceptance during onboarding and continues until terminated by either party on fourteen days' written notice.

12.2 CK LLC may terminate this Agreement immediately if you lose required health permits, fail to maintain insurance, cause a food safety risk, change your IAE classification, or materially breach any term.

13. Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, United States of America. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wyoming, USA. Physical food execution on-site remains subject to mandatory local Host Jurisdiction public health regulations, for which you assume sole liability. Nothing in this clause limits your rights under any mandatory applicable laws of your own jurisdiction.
