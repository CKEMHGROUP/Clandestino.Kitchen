Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Version 1.0

CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Who We Are

The data controller responsible for your personal data is CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC, registered in the State of Wyoming, United States of America, operating as Clandestino Kitchen, JoEs TaBLe, and Pipeline by CK.

Data enquiries: compliance@clandestino.kitchen

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: name, company name, job title
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, postal address
  • Transaction data: details of services purchased, payments made, invoices issued
  • Communications data: messages sent to us via our website, email, or other channels
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (via cookies and analytics tools)
  • Event data: dietary requirements, attendance, preferences provided in connection with event bookings

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To fulfil service agreements and process bookings or orders
  • To communicate with you regarding your enquiry or engagement
  • To issue invoices and process payments
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To send relevant communications where you have given consent or where we have a legitimate business interest

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to deliver services you have engaged us for
  • Legitimate interests: operating and improving our business, preventing fraud, communicating with prospective clients
  • Legal obligation: compliance with applicable laws and regulations
  • Consent: where you have explicitly opted in to receive communications from us

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers acting as data processors on our behalf (e.g. payment processors, email platforms, cloud storage)
  • Professional advisors including lawyers, accountants, and compliance consultants, under confidentiality obligations
  • Regulatory authorities or law enforcement where required by law
  • Operational partners (chefs, venues, producers) strictly as necessary to deliver an event or service you have booked, and only with your knowledge

6. International Transfers

As a US-registered entity operating internationally, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. Where data is transferred internationally, we apply appropriate safeguards in line with applicable data protection law, including standard contractual clauses where required.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Financial and transactional records are retained for a minimum of seven years in accordance with US and applicable local tax law. Event booking records are retained for three years after the event date.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at compliance@clandestino.kitchen. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to support basic functionality and, where consent is given, for analytics purposes. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published at clandestino.kitchen/privacy. Material changes will be communicated where we hold your contact details.
