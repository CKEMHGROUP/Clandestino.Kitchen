Global Client & Services Framework Agreement (GCSA)

GLOBAL MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — VERSION 3.1

This framework agreement establishes the master legal and operational principles governing collaborations between CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC and the partner entity identified in Step 1.

1. Independent Entity Status: The partner operates strictly as an independent B2B contractor. Nothing herein shall construct an employment, agency, or partnership relationship.

2. Permits & Compliance: The partner retains full operational autonomy and 100% legal responsibility for maintaining all food safety, alcohol, health, tax, and local business permits required in their operational jurisdiction.

3. Cross-Border Invoicing: All invoices issued to CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC (Wyoming, USA) must reflect complete legal business registration numbers and appropriate tax handling per the partner's jurisdiction.

4. Confidentiality & Brand Standards: Event concepts, client lists, menus, and operational documentation disclosed by CK LLC remain strict intellectual property.

5. Service Orders: Individual activations, schedules, and deliverables are executed under standalone, project-specific Service Orders subject to these overarching master terms.
