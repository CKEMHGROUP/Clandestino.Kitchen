Chef Services Agreement — Type A

Applies to: independent culinary professionals engaged per event

GLOBAL MASTER CHEF SERVICES AGREEMENT (TYPE A) — VERSION 3.1

Ref: GCSA-2026-GLOBAL  ·  Electronic Onboarding Click-Through Agreement


RECITALS & ELECTRONIC ACCEPTANCE
This Global Master Chef Services Agreement ("Agreement") establishes a binding legal framework between CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC (trading as Clandestino Kitchen and JoEs TaBLe), a limited liability company incorporated in the State of Wyoming, USA, and you as the independent culinary contractor ("the Chef") identified in Step 1 of this onboarding submission. By checking the acceptance box below, you electronically consent and agree to all terms set forth herein, which shall govern all future Service Orders issued to you by CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC.

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1. An "Event" means a corporate experience or culinary activation delivered under the JoEs TaBLe brand anywhere in the world, as confirmed in a Service Order.
1.2. A "Service Order" means a written or electronic document issued by CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC specifying the event date, host country/jurisdiction, venue, guest count, brief, agreed fee, and operational parameters for an Event.
1.3. "Host Jurisdiction" means the specific country, state, territory, province, or municipality where physical food preparation or service execution takes place.
1.4. "Brand Standards" means concept presentation guidelines, plating aesthetics, service conduct, and experiential narratives communicated by CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES & ABSOLUTE OPERATIONAL SEPARATION
2.1. Independent Engagement: You are engaged solely as an independent professional contractor providing B2B culinary consultation, menu execution, and event direction directly to CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC. You do not provide services to, nor maintain any direct commercial or contractual relationship with, any end guest, attendee, or event client.
2.2. Exclusion of Facilities & Logistics: CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC does not own, lease, operate, manage, supervise, or inspect any kitchen, food preparation space, cold storage, transport vehicle, or culinary equipment in any Host Jurisdiction. You are solely and exclusively responsible for securing, inspecting, and operating fully licensed, sanitary, and legally compliant production premises and equipment.

3. GLOBAL HEALTH, SANITARY & FOOD SAFETY COMPLIANCE
3.1. Health & Hygiene Standards: You warrant that all ingredient sourcing, transportation, cold-chain maintenance, storage, preparation, cooking, holding, allergen management, and service strictly comply with: (a) all applicable public health and food hygiene laws of the Host Jurisdiction; (b) international hygiene standards (HACCP / APPCC protocols); and (c) mandatory allergen disclosure regulations applicable in the Host Jurisdiction (including EU Regulation 1169/2011 or local equivalents).
3.2. Universal Certification Warranty: You warrant that you and all members of your culinary team hold valid food handler certificates, health permits, and professional registrations required by the Host Jurisdiction. You shall upload or supply copies of these certificates upon request.
3.3. Traceability: You retain sole operational responsibility for food temperature logs, allergen cross-contamination prevention, dietary restriction enforcement, and ingredient origin traceability records.

4. BRAND STANDARDS vs. OPERATIONAL AUTONOMY
4.1. Scope: CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC’s Brand Standards govern exclusively visual aesthetics, concept narrative, plating alignment, service timing, and guest experience design.
4.2. Total Independence: Brand Standards do not control or direct—and shall never be interpreted as controlling or directing—kitchen operations, food safety procedures, raw ingredient selection, sanitation, or physical food production mechanics. You retain complete operational autonomy over all food safety practices.

5. TOTAL LIABILITY ALLOCATION, INDEMNITY & HOLD HARMLESS
5.1. Sole Liability: You retain 100% exclusive legal, civil, criminal, regulatory, and financial liability for food safety, toxic food reactions, foodborne illnesses, allergic reactions, kitchen accidents, personal injury, physical property damage, and sanitary infractions arising from your services.
5.2. Absolute Indemnification: To the maximum extent permitted by law, you shall defend, indemnify, and hold completely harmless CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC, its officers, directors, members, employees, agents, and brand affiliates from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, penalties, fines, administrative sanctions, and legal costs (including attorney fees) arising from: (a) your negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct; (b) food poisoning, allergic reactions, or injury suffered by any guest or third party; (c) failure to maintain required health permits, tax registrations, or insurance; (d) damage to venue premises or equipment; or (e) any labor, health, or tax audit/fine brought by local authorities in any Host Jurisdiction relating to your services.
5.3. Limitation of Liability: Under no circumstances shall CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC’s total aggregate liability arising out of or related to any Service Order shall not exceed the direct contract fee paid to you under that specific Service Order.

6. INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS
6.1. Mandatory Policy: You shall maintain in full force: (a) Public & General Liability Insurance covering third-party bodily injury, property damage, and foodborne product liability with a minimum limit of EUR 300,000 / USD 350,000 (or local equivalent) per occurrence; and (b) Professional Indemnity Insurance.
6.2. Proof: You shall supply an active Certificate of Insurance naming CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC as an Additional Insured upon request prior to performing under any Service Order.

7. TAX STATUS AND CROSS-BORDER INVOICING
7.1. CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is incorporated in Wyoming, USA, with no permanent establishment outside the United States. Services rendered by you are B2B professional services supplied to a US entity.
7.2. You shall invoice CK LLC in accordance with the cross-border invoicing rules applicable in your own jurisdiction for services supplied to a US recipient. CK LLC will not act as a tax withholding agent in any jurisdiction. You are solely responsible for the correct tax treatment of your invoices and for declaring and paying all taxes applicable to your income in your home jurisdiction. The correct treatment is your responsibility — verify with your own accountant before issuing.
7.3. Invoices to CK LLC shall include your full legal name, your tax identification number, your registered address, the service description referencing the relevant Concept Experience, the agreed fee, and your applicable tax treatment. CK LLC will provide its EIN upon request for your compliance records.

8. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR & LABOR SHIELD
8.1. Status: You act strictly as an independent contractor. Nothing herein creates an employment, agency, joint venture, or statutory partnership relationship.
8.2. Subcontractor & Staff Insulation: Any assistants, sub-chefs, or kitchen staff engaged by you for an Event are exclusively employed or contracted by you. You assume 100% legal, financial, and administrative responsibility for their compensation, workplace safety, social security contributions, tax withholdings, and work permits. CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC is completely shielded from joint labor, social security, or cesión ilegal de trabajadores claims globally.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY
9.1. IP Ownership: All Menu Concepts, recipe pairings, tasting narratives, and presentation formats developed by you for JoEs TaBLe Events are the exclusive intellectual property of CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC globally from creation.
9.2. NDA: You shall keep strictly confidential all client details, pricing models, guest identities, and operational procedures, and shall not publish photos or brand references without prior written authorization.

10. TERM, TERMINATION & GOVERNING LAW
10.1. Term: Commences upon electronic acceptance during onboarding and continues until terminated by either party upon fourteen (14) days' written notice.
10.2. Immediate Termination: CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC may terminate this Agreement immediately if you lose required health permits, fail to maintain insurance, cause a sanitary/food safety risk, or materially breach any term.
10.3. Governing Law: This Agreement is governed strictly by the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA, with exclusive jurisdiction in the State or Federal Courts located in Wyoming, USA. Physical food execution on-site remains subject to local mandatory Host Jurisdiction public health regulations for which you assume 100% sole liability.

Venue Partnership Agreement

Applies to: independently licensed venue operators

This Venue Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is between CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC ("CK LLC"), trading as JoEs TaBLe, and you as the venue operator ("the Venue Partner") identified in this onboarding submission.

You agree to make your licensed premises available for JoEs TaBLe Concept Experiences on dates confirmed via Service Order. You shall provide qualified on-site service staff and kitchen access to the culinary team appointed by CK LLC.

You shall obtain and maintain all licences, permits, and certifications required by applicable law for the operation of a food service establishment, including but not limited to the applicable operating licence in your jurisdiction. CK LLC bears no responsibility for any regulatory compliance obligation relating to your premises or food service operation.

CK LLC's Brand Standards govern concept presentation, table setting, and guest experience. They do not govern the structural condition of the premises, venue safety, fire safety compliance, food service hygiene, or any matter that is the subject of your autonomous regulatory obligations. Compliance with all applicable regulations governing licensed premises is your sole and exclusive responsibility.

You shall indemnify and hold harmless CK LLC from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, costs, and liabilities arising from the condition of your premises; any failure to maintain required licences or certifications; any act or omission of your staff at an event; or any claim by a guest or third party arising from the physical condition or operation of your premises.

Client relationships, bookings, and all correspondence with clients are the exclusive property of CK LLC. You shall not contact clients directly without prior written consent of CK LLC. You agree not to directly solicit or accept bookings from clients introduced to you through JoEs TaBLe Concept Experiences during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve months following termination.

All intellectual property rights in the JoEs TaBLe brand, concept, and associated materials are owned exclusively by CK LLC. Events delivered at your premises operate exclusively under the JoEs TaBLe brand. You shall not co-brand or present the experience under your own trading name without prior written consent.

This Agreement commences on acceptance and continues until terminated by either party on thirty days' written notice. CK LLC may terminate immediately if you lose or fail to maintain any required licence, or if you fail to maintain required insurance cover.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, United States of America. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Wyoming, USA. Nothing in this clause limits your rights under any mandatory applicable laws of your own jurisdiction.
