GLOBAL MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — VERSION 3.1


This agreement establishes the legal framework between CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC and the contracting partner identified in Step 1.


1. Independent Entity Status: The partner acts strictly as an independent legal contractor. Nothing herein shall be construed to create an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the parties.


2. Regulatory Compliance: The partner retains 100% operational autonomy and total responsibility for local health, tax, labor, and operational permits required in any jurisdiction where services are performed.


3. Invoicing & Cross-Border Tax: Invoices issued to CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC (Wyoming, USA) must reflect valid legal identity data, compliant invoicing numbers, and tax handling applicable to cross-border service transactions.


4. Confidentiality & Brand Standard: Proprietary guest lists, event concepts, client details, and menu formulations shared during production remain the exclusive intellectual property of CK Events Management & Hospitality Group LLC.


5. Execution of Service Orders: Specific schedules, deliverables, fees, and logistics will be executed via standalone project-specific Service Orders subject to this framework.
